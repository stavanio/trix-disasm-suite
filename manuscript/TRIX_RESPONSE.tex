\section*{Overview of the revision}

We changed the title from ``TRiX: Neuro-Symbolic Safety for Foundation Model
Agents in Robotic Disassembly'' to ``TRiX: Task-conditioned execution
governance for robotic disassembly'', because no autonomous agent loop was
evaluated; the model study governs recorded proposals.

\textbf{Correction of the submitted evidence.} The submitted headline policy
results came from random-action stubs, not trained policies. We withdraw those
results and their policy-learning interpretation, replacing them with recorded
trained-policy experiments and corrected task implementations and outcome
definitions. The disclosure preceding R4.1 details these defects; the note to
Reviewer 2 names the withdrawn headline claims.

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
The main changes are:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.3em]
\item Recorded PPO and SAC policies (ten seeds, held-out evaluation), a matched
QP control, 200 held-out procedural geometries and specification controls,
separating effects the submission conflated.
\item A frozen-policy study: with the admissible set fixed, rescaling one
command scale in the projection metric lowers PPO SCREW safe completion from
60.0\% to as low as 20.0\%, while every forwarded command stays admissible.
\item Four representation and feasibility propositions replace the submitted
ISS theorem; policy adaptation is reported as an empirical finding.
\item Actual VLM outputs: of 257 grounded command pairs from 273 decisions on
91 PCB images, TRiX prevents all 31 recorded constraint-violation cases and
introduces none.
\item Physical intervention evidence on a six-degree-of-freedom Seeed reBot
B601-RS arm (three video-documented trials, three logged repetitions).
\item A prospectively declared shifted-reset evaluation: in SNAP's
outside-support shell, separately trained TRiX and static systems reach
59.9\% and 3.5\% safe completion, yet fractures occur in 401 and 468 of 1,000
episodes per arm. This is a system comparison, not an isolated governor effect.
\item Mapped source and evidence, seed-level uncertainty, disclosed provenance
limits, and a restructured manuscript with a redrawn Figure~1.
\end{enumerate}
